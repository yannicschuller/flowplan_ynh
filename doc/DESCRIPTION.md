Flowplan is an open-source alternative to Notion and AppFlowy: documents, databases, whiteboards and a daily journal in one page tree, edited together and live.

### Features

- **Documents** with blocks, tables, tasks, math, code, Mermaid diagrams, handwriting and comments on passages
- **Databases** with table, board, calendar, timeline, gallery and list views, formulas, relations, forms and recurring records
- **Whiteboards** with shapes, sticky notes, connectors and freehand drawing
- **Journal** with daily pages, trackers, reviews and tasks
- **Live collaboration**, version history, sharing and publishing pages on the web
- **Apps** for iPhone, iPad, Android, Windows, macOS and Linux that connect to your server
- Import from Notion, AFFiNE, Obsidian, Markdown and Office files; export to Markdown, PDF and archives
- **YunoHost integration:** sign in with your YunoHost account, and automatic sign-in when you are logged in on the YunoHost portal
